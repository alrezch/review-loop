# The bar: landing page

## What it's for

A one-page site for a local business. A visitor on a phone should understand what the business does and contact it within 10 seconds.

## Checks

- [ ] The headline says what the business does in under 10 words.
- [ ] The main button (call, WhatsApp or form) is visible without scrolling on a 390 px wide screen.
- [ ] Every link and button works; none points to `#` or a placeholder.
- [ ] No console errors on load.
- [ ] Every word on the page is in the HTML the server sends (View Source), not only added by JavaScript, so search engines and AI tools can read it.
- [ ] Images have alt text and none is over 300 KB.
- [ ] The page title and description are filled in and specific to this business.
- [ ] Nothing overflows sideways on a phone.

## Never

- Lorem ipsum or "Your text here".
- A contact form that doesn't actually send anywhere.
