# The bar: client email

## What it's for

An email to a client that they can read on their phone and act on without replying with questions.

## Checks

- [ ] Under 150 words.
- [ ] The first sentence says why you're writing.
- [ ] Exactly one thing is asked of the client, and it's clear by when.
- [ ] Every name, date, amount and link is correct against the source (the brief, the invoice, the calendar).
- [ ] No jargon the client wouldn't use themselves.

## Never

- "Just checking in" with nothing new to say.
- An attachment mentioned but not attached.
